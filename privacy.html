Privacy policy

coach is a personal app with exactly one user: its owner.

  • What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's own Google Health data (sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, activity, weight).
  • How it's used: solely to maintain the owner's personal training and health log.
  • Where it's stored: in a private repository controlled by the owner.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
